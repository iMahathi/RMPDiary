RMP Diary Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · App: RMP Diary (package com.doctor.rmpclinic) · Contact: contact.imahathi@gmail.com

The short version

RMP Diary is a record book for doctors and Registered Medical Practitioners (RMPs). You sign in with your phone number. The patient records, bills, payments and notes you enter are stored on your phone and backed up to your own private space in Google Firebase, so that you can use them on a new phone. Only you can read that space. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data for advertising or marketing.

Who this policy is for

The app is used by doctors ("you"). You enter information about your patients. For that patient information, you decide what is recorded and why, and you are responsible for having your patients' consent where the law requires it, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We store and process it only to provide the app to you.

Where it is stored

Records are saved on your phone first, so the app works without internet. When you are online, they are copied to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), a service run by Google, in a space that belongs to your account. Access rules let only your signed-in account read or change it. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and Google encrypts it at rest. Crash reports are processed by Firebase Crashlytics. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

Permissions

  • Contacts: when you tap to add a patient from your contacts, you choose one contact and the app reads only that contact's name, phone numbers and photo. Your contact list is not uploaded.
  • Microphone: used only while you tap the mic to dictate a note or symptoms. Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google), which may process the audio under its own privacy policy. RMP Diary keeps only the text, never a recording.
  • Camera: to take a patient or logo photo, the app opens your phone's camera app. The app does not use the camera in the background.
  • Notifications, alarms and restart: used to show your reminders at the right time, also after the phone restarts. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Internet and network state: to sign in, back up your records, and show whether syncing has finished.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information leaves the app only in these cases:

  • When you share it: sending a bill on WhatsApp, saving or sharing a PDF, or exporting a CSV file. You choose the app or place it goes to, and that app's privacy policy applies from then on.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase stores your records and crash reports on our behalf, as described above.
  • The law: if a valid legal request requires it.

Demo mode

Demo mode shows made-up sample patients. Demo data is kept only on your phone, is never uploaded, and is deleted when you leave demo mode.

How long we keep data

Your records are kept for as long as your account exists, so your history is there when you need it. Notes and price-list items you delete in the app are removed from your phone and from Firebase. To remove patient, treatment or payment records, delete your account as described below. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and everything stored with it (profile, patients, treatments, bills, payments, notes and photos) yourself, in the app:

  1. Open Settings › Delete account. You need an internet connection.
  2. Confirm. If you signed in some time ago, the app sends a new OTP to your mobile number to check that it is you.
  3. The app deletes your photos, all your records in Firebase and your sign-in account straight away, then removes the copy on your phone. Backup copies kept by Google are removed on Google's normal schedule.

Before deleting, you can export your records from Settings (CSV). The delete screen offers this too. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app, ask us by email instead:

  1. Email contact.imahathi@gmail.com with the subject "RMP Diary account deletion".
  2. Include the mobile number you sign in with. We may ask you to confirm the request from that number.
  3. We delete the account and all its data from Firebase within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Uninstalling the app or signing out removes the copy on your phone but does not delete your backed-up records.

Your rights

You can see and correct your information in the app at any time, export it, or ask us to delete it. Patients who want to see, correct or delete their information should ask their doctor, who controls those records in the app. You can also contact us at the address below.

Children

RMP Diary is a tool for medical practitioners and is not meant for anyone under 18. Patient records may include children, entered by their doctor.

Medical disclaimer

RMP Diary is a record-keeping and billing tool. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment or give medical advice.
